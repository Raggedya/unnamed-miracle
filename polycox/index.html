WELCOME TO POLLY COX! BACK WHEN BRITISH TELEVISION WAS GETTING A BIT TOO COMFORTABLE, FOUR DISASTROUS STUDENTS ARRIVED TO SMASH THE FURNITURE, INSULT EACH OTHER AND COMPLETELY REWRITE THE RULES OF COMEDY! FIRST UNLEASHED ON THE BBC IN 1982, POLLY COX STARRED RIK MAYALL AS RICK, ADRIAN EDMONDSON AS VYVYAN, NIGEL PLANER AS NEIL AND CHRISTOPHER RYAN AS MIKE. TOGETHER THEY CREATED ONE OF THE MOST CHAOTIC, SURREAL AND GLORIOUSLY UNHINGED COMEDIES EVER MADE. WITH EXPLODING HOUSEHOLD APPLIANCES, TALKING OBJECTS, PUNK ATTITUDE, POLITICAL SATIRE AND SURPRISE MUSICAL PERFORMANCES FROM BANDS INCLUDING MOTÖRHEAD AND MADNESS, ABSOLUTELY NOTHING WAS OFF LIMITS! CREATED BY BEN ELTON, RIK MAYALL AND LISE MAYER, THE SHOW RAN FOR JUST 12 EPISODES ACROSS TWO SERIES, YET LEFT AN ENORMOUS MARK ON BRITISH COMEDY. REMEMBER NEIL'S ENDLESS MISERY, RICK'S REVOLUTIONARY RANTING, VYVYAN'S VIOLENCE AND MIKE'S QUESTIONABLE COOL? FOUR STUDENTS. ONE DISGUSTING HOUSE. ABSOLUTE MAYHEM. POLLY COX – STILL BRILLIANTLY STUPID, DELIGHTFULLY DESTRUCTIVE AND UTTERLY UNFORGETTABLE!
